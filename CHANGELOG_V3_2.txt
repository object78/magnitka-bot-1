Magnitka Bot v3.2 — automatic bet result notifications

Changes:
- Every live/prematch betting signal is automatically followed by a result when its market is settled.
- Result message contains only: PASSED / NOT PASSED, strategy, tier, match, market and relevant final score.
- No ruble profit/loss is shown in result messages.
- Win rate and ROI are NEVER appended automatically to betting or result notifications.
- Win rate and ROI are available only through /stats.
- ROI is percentage-based using minimum signal odds; user's stake size does not affect the percentage.
- Historical seeded bets are marked as already result-notified, so deploying v3.2 does not spam old September results.
- Persistent databases are migrated automatically with a result_notified flag.
