Magnitka Telegram Bot v3 — Paid Access + Performance
====================================================
- Closed access: /start without invite grants nothing.
- Owner-only admin via OWNER_TELEGRAM_ID.
- One-time invite links for 7/14/30 day subscriptions.
- Invite activation window configurable with INVITE_VALID_HOURS (24h default).
- Automatic subscription expiry; expired users stop receiving broadcasts at exact expiry.
- 24h expiry reminder.
- Owner commands: /invite7 /invite14 /invite30 /users /extend /revoke.
- User read-only commands: /stats /subscription.
- Actual bet signals are persisted to SQLite and automatically settled.
- Rolling 7-day and 30-day win rate + ROI, overall and per strategy.
- Compact 7/30 performance footer on new actual bet signals.
- ROI uses signal minimum odds and FLAT_STAKE, not user's actual bookmaker price.
- Live Fix v2 retained.
